# Opening UI Gap Assessment

The connected private product opening offers ordinary New Game, field preparation, Search/pickups,
required Church services, Battle01 and usable return, with explicit Save/Quit and fresh-process Continue.
The [composed acceptance](opening-loop-acceptance.md) records its state/resource and resumed-play boundary.
Remaining gaps include preferences beyond the four delivered settings, services beyond the admitted opening, and
whole-opening human/aesthetic approval. A–E now have accepted bounded deliveries: world scale,
walking/Talk, story presentation, menu mouse input, battle status/messages/return and read-only Party
Status. Their engineering acceptance does not establish full-game or all-device experience.

## Church and shared-save calibration (#1519)

**Standard, bounded candidate:** the existing priest Save already completed the real caller and
opened the same save host. The [current agreement](../godot/presentation.md#church-and-shared-save-agreement-1519)

its selector meaning. A controlled-profile refusal now states its true unavailability instead of
asking to finish an already-ended conversation. A paused Church's early return skipped the ordinary
neutral-input release reset, canceling old gestures but also blocking fresh mouse clicks; its bounded
branch correction retains the old-gesture guard and restores fresh pointer input after release.

independent integration; headless state/input/font/geometry is not final pixel, physical focus/device
or human acceptance. E8 remains **experience pending**. Preserve existing shutdown Unknowns and
completed historical failures; no whole-route acceptance or new Church service is claimed.

Evidence labels have their repository meanings:

- **Confirmed:** a named source establishes structure, or a linked retained observation establishes
  its stated runtime boundary. Static confirmation is explicitly qualified as structure.
- **Inferred:** a likely user impact or interpretation that lacks independent experience confirmation.
- **Unknown:** an unobserved experience, unresolved product choice or unsupported route; no pass is implied.

Delivery states are separate from evidence labels:

| State | Meaning in this assessment |
| --- | --- |
| **Not implemented** | No player-facing consumer in the inspected ordinary route; this can coexist with useful underlying data. |
| **Available, not integrated** | A resource, interface or candidate exists but is not connected to accepted ordinary play for this purpose. |
| **Integrated, experience pending** | Accepted code connects the behavior; present evidence does not establish its readability/discoverability or user approval. |
| **Accepted, bounded** | Existing independent engineering acceptance covers the named behavior, with retained limits. It does not imply approval of the whole UI. |
| **Experienced and accepted, bounded** | The user actually accepted the named scope; retain its historical evidence rather than extending it to later changes. |

recommendations, not a new implementation queue: **P1** affects ordinary control or understanding of
the connected route; **P2** is a later consistency/usability improvement or a product decision before
implementation. No new P0 is established. Delivered slices A–E are defined below; an Epic link means
the remaining human review belongs to #1456 unless a narrower owner is named.
