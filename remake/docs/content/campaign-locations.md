# Campaign locations from New Game through first Pacalon

Use this index to find what changes at a place, how the party enters or leaves, and what a return
or Continue restores. It covers New Game through the drought-state Pacalon town and castle and
the departure onto Map71 after Battle26, before Battle27. This is the location slice of the
[Standard-rigor campaign lifecycle agreement](../../../docs/design/documentation-roadmap.md#campaign-lifecycle-indexes).
Use the [item index](campaign-items.md) for custody and effects, the [flag index](campaign-flags.md)
for writers and lifetimes, and the [feature trace](../capability-status.md#campaign-feature-trace)
for implemented actions and their acceptance.

The [tracked selection](../../content/private/pre-voyage-opening.json) has **50 distinct map IDs**
in `maps`, including scene and encounter maps. Of those, 25 IDs have explicit additional
`setups` entries and 42 appear in `searchMaps`. The Map IDs column assigns each selected ID
to one row. Shared maps and areas are cross-referenced wherever they serve another route.
The IDs are decimal. This count measures the package selection. The location rows identify
the narrower boundaries of conditional entry, usable services and actual play. The
[field Search inventory](../application/campaign-field-search.md#map-and-setup-inventory) describes its
first-raft selection at that chapter's checkpoint. Follow later chapter owners for subsequent additions.

The original column summarizes **Confirmed** source behavior at the linked static or observed
boundary. **Inferred** marks a source-supported interpretation and **Unknown** an unresolved
claim. The remake column identifies ordinary inputs where observed and calls out controlled
starts, compiled-only behavior and unavailable paths. Original natural reach and hardware timing
remain **Unknown** where a linked source establishes only the tables or callback.
