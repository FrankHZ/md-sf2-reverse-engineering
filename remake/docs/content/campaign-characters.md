fight, and what happens when they leave or the player returns. It covers the opening through
first Pacalon town and castle and departure onto Map71 after Battle26. Start with the
[location index](campaign-locations.md) for the route, the [item index](campaign-items.md) for
custody and effects, or the [flag index](campaign-flags.md#joined-active-and-following) for the
state writers and readers. The [feature trace](../capability-status.md#campaign-feature-trace)
routes current player actions to their acceptance evidence.

**Confirmed** in the original column means the linked owner establishes the stated source
condition or bounded observation. Natural original traversal, presentation timing and original
save restoration remain **Unknown** wherever the evidence stops at source or a handler probe.
The remake column identifies ordinary observations, controlled checks and unconnected branches.

for the general rules behind the character rows. A joined bit records force membership. Active
selection, eligibility to take a turn, control and the presence of a map actor each have their own
state.
