# Campaign items through first Pacalon

Find an item by name to trace its pickup, actual pack or storage location, use and save boundary.
The connected route starts at New Game and ends at the first drought-state Pacalon town and castle
visit, including departure to Map71 after Battle26 and before Battle27. Later consumers are linked where they explain
an item already available or listed on the route. See the [campaign feature trace](../capability-status.md#campaign-feature-trace)
for the journeys and the [flag index](campaign-flags.md#pickup-receipts-and-custody) for receipts.

## Custody and save rules

- **Field Search.** The original checks the faced working tile's marker and current receipt. The
  first joined member with room receives the item. A full-party refusal leaves a retry available
  (the original chest path can open and then roll back). The remake publishes the grant, receipt
  and any chest block together. Search after a successful claim is empty even if the item was
  moved, dropped or used. Rows that say *selected* have a content binding. Ordinary access and
  collection are **Unknown** unless the linked chapter identifies a witness. [Original Search](../../../docs/research/world/field-search-control.md) ·
  [Remake map and marker inventory](../application/campaign-field-search.md#map-and-setup-inventory) ·
  [Current campaign selection](../../content/private/pre-voyage-opening.json) ·
  [FieldSearch](../../src/Sf2.Remake.Application/Runtime/Exploration/FieldSearch.cs)
- **Packs, transfers and storage.** For Search rewards and transferable items below, use the
  actual joined member and slot, including reserves. The remake's field Give can exchange with a
  full pack. The Caravan Depot can hold 64 items and retrieve to space or exchange with a full pack.
  Deposit removes the item from the pack and stores its normalized identity. Later story checks
  for a held item search joined packs and skip depot stock and Deals. Drop can refuse unsellable
  items and route eligible rare items to Deals, which has a separate per-item limit. Apply each
  service's guards, including its equipment and curse rules. [Original services](../../../docs/design/contracts/party/service-interactions.md#caravan-source-boundary) ·
  [Remake Depot](../application/caravan-services-design.md#current-depot-implementation) ·
  [Remake Item](../application/caravan-services-design.md#current-item-implementation) ·
  [Deals buyback and stock #1947](https://github.com/FrankHZ/md-sf2-reverse-engineering/issues/1947)
- **Battle drop.** For the drops below, the battle, enemy slot, actual held identity and
  qualifying ally action have to match. The acting ally receives the item unequipped if alive
  with room. A failed delivery loses ordinary items and can send rare items to Deals within its
  cap. A set once bit blocks another receipt. A leader cleanup which sweeps the living carrier
  gives no action drop. Battle once bits use their own bank separate from story flags. [Original drop table and consumer](../../../docs/research/battle/battle-global-data.md) ·
  [Remake drop rules](../domain/gameplay-rules.md#finite-physical-action-item-drops) ·
  [Connected encounter bindings](../../src/Sf2.Remake.Content/Scenarios/PrivateBattleScenarioReader.cs#L107-L117)
- **Save.** At admitted remake field and battle save boundaries, carried packs (including
  equipment), story receipts, drop bits, Deals and depot persist as separate data. An absent
  depot in compatible older payloads restores as empty. A save witness applies to the named
  journey and outcome. A receipt alone cannot answer who holds an item, whether it is still
  carried, or whether a use effect was ever applied. [Field recipes](../application/opening-session-lifecycle.md#eligibility-and-restoration-recipes) ·
  [Battle continuation](../application/battle-save-and-continue.md#implemented-domainapplication-boundary-a) ·
  [Depot persistence](../application/caravan-services-design.md#current-depot-implementation)

In the original column, **Confirmed** refers to the cited source, ROM or bounded owner evidence.
Hardware timing, natural traversal of static branches and original save outcomes remain
**Unknown** wherever the owner lacks that evidence. The remake column distinguishes observed
journeys from current bindings. An item can be selected for carry without a player Equip or Use
effect. [Original item definitions](../../../docs/design/contracts/party/item-definition-data.md) ·
