## Search and exits

**Confirmed** from the pinned Map48 entries. The northern section has a Fairy Tear chest at (34,4), F168. The separate western section has a Silver Tank at (24,8), F146. The map has no non-chest item rows or area descriptions. The northern section has two field exits, (55,30) to Map72 (27,34) and (52,13) to Map72 (25,29), both Down. The other section uses (15,27) and (3,12). The north mouth’s reverse warp at Map72 (25,28) requests Map48 (52,12), Up. Reproduce the inventory with the pinned SF2DISASM files under `disasm/data/maps/entries/map48/`, particularly `2-areas.asm`, `6-warp-events.asm`, `7-chest-items.asm` and `8-other-items.asm`.

**Confirmed** by static inspection for the USA battle menu. In `battlefunctions_2.asm` the faced tile reaches `CheckChestItem`. In `exploration.asm:GetItem`, Battle25 indexes the seven-byte camera table with stride five. Its lookup offsets are (12,0). Only local (12,8) and (22,4) could match these two item rows. Their cropped layout words at Map48 (40,8) and (50,4) are 0x0031 and 0x0012. Both fail the 0x1800 chest marker. The existing Battle15 correction is specific to its shrine. In field exploration the battle offset is skipped and the marker at (34,4) is present. [Battle15 exception](east-shrine-panel.md#optional-battle-chest) · [Field Search](campaign-field-search.md)

**Confirmed** in an optional English round trip from a copy of the north-mouth save. Re-entering from the north gave fresh Map48 field control. Facing Up from (34,5), field Search granted Fairy Tear 7 to Bowie, set F168 and made the next Search empty. The party used (52,13) to return to the overworld. ## Reproduction

Use the private issue 1918 handoff and the registered worktree configuration. `Rebuild.ps1 -Repo <checkout> -Output <fresh-external-directory>` prepares this encounter from the registered ROM, pinned source, predecessor package and language corpus. The generated world is 100,738,102 bytes. The handoff carries its own runtime, adapter, catalog, encounter media and receipt namespaces.

From the package run `./Play.ps1 -Language en -Mode entry-west -Sample <fresh-name>` or `./Play.ps1 -Language zh-CN -Mode entry -Sample <fresh-name>`. Both begin at a copy of the corresponding ordinary opened-cliff save. For the English witness the Caravan met the party after the west scroll. The Chinese party walked onto the Caravan at the cliff. Both entered through the same warp. For a saved fight, add `-Seed <battle-slot>` and select `-Mode action` for the first Rohde shot, `escape` for the paid retreat, or `win` to play to the actual outcome and save. A defeat also leaves a town save. Use `-Mode retry -Seed <Hassan-slot>` to raise the party and return. The receipts retain the actual roster, costs, targets, moves and terminal coordinates.

For independent endpoint verification, run `./Play.ps1 -Language en -Mode continue -Sample <fresh-name>` and repeat in `zh-CN`. Use `-Mode chest -Seed <north-mouth-slot>` for the optional fresh re-entry. Omitting `-Sample` gives the player normal interactive control. The next campaign boundary is Ketto.

The same configured SDK can run the engine test project with `--filter 'FullyQualifiedName~EnemySpellDecisionTests|FullyQualifiedName~TargetSelectionTests'`. The package handoff gives the commands for the broader affected checks, adapter build, independent play and title comparison. Human audiovisual acceptance, hardware timing, alternate lead-member victories, Angel Wing retreat in this chapter and exhaustive optional variants remain **Unknown**. Some native exits have the existing [ObjectDB warning](https://github.com/FrankHZ/md-sf2-reverse-engineering/issues/1716).
