# New Granseal, Ribble and the eastern cave approach

The ordinary private campaign advances the [Battle08 founding party](battle08-new-granseal.md) through two battles to the road to the Dark Caves. The East Shrine interior is playable from its own entrance. The field map retains the original land and warp conditions. Battle11 begins the next slice.

## Play

In the prepared checkout run `./local/issue1736/play.ps1 zh-CN Castle` or `./local/issue1736/play.ps1 en Castle`, then choose Continue and slot 2. The castle slot starts at first walking control after the year-later sequence. To see that sequence from its predecessor, select `Voyage` and Continue in slot 1. `Ribble` starts before recruiting May and `Cave` starts at the natural Battle10 return, both in slot 1. Each entry and language uses its own player namespace. Movement, interaction, battle and Save use the ordinary game controls.

1. Advance the founding and year-later messages, including 1290 and the audience in the castle. At F707 the player can walk on Map7 (11,7) facing Up. Walk south into town, visit the children and Peter, then walk north to the castle audience. After the return to town Peter follows in the field and acts automatically in combat.
2. Leave town to Map73, cross east onto Map72 and travel south into Map69. Play Battle09 with the current living active party. After winning, walk to Ribble at (44,7). Enter town, advance the confrontation and enter the mayor's house from (14,10). The mayor's story allows departure. Talk to May in town to recruit her, or leave her there. The next battle does not require her.
3. Leave Ribble south. Travel east to Map70 and play Battle10. The observed winning party returned to Map70 (17,19) facing Down. Position and facing come from the battle return. From this return, travel north onto Map71, west onto Map72 and walk to (58,50), one tile south of the cave mouth. This is the playable handoff to #1737. The cave approach uses neither the shrine visit nor its follower flags.
