## From the west stair to the battlefield

From the southern Fairy Cave chest, take the west stair at (2,3). On first arrival at (51,23), walk Left onto (50,23). With F521 clear the zone sets F421 and the Map67 selector admits Battle21. F471 records that the before/start sequence has run. After EGRESS, the cave exit itself can satisfy the already-unlocked selector. Both parties saved at the first player Movement. After a cold Continue, confirming movement opened the action menu. Cancelling and saving again reproduced each complete battle payload. Formation reads current membership and health.
