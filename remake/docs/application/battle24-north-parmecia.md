# New Granseal to the North Cliff approach

Continue the ordinary [New Granseal exit](new-granseal-return.md) on Map73, cross east onto Map72 and enter the northern road encounter. On victory the party can walk to the south side of the cliff and save. The [North Cliff chapter](north-cliff-rohde.md) consumes these saves for the explosion and Rohde's recruitment. High rigor applies because the next chapter consumes this party's actual battle outcome, inventory and location.

## Reproduction and remaining coverage

Load the checkout's registered private configuration. In the private issue 1904 handoff, run `Rebuild.ps1 -Repo <checkout> -Output <fresh-external-directory>` to generate the selected world, battle, display catalogs and adapter. The input is the registered USA retail ROM with the project's pinned SF2DISASM revision. Generation is confined to one encounter plus the existing campaign world. The world payload is approximately 101 MB and has a 128 MiB publication limit. Historical samples, tool binaries and extracted content stay private.

Use `Play.ps1 -Language en` or `-Language zh-CN` for ordinary control from the corresponding immutable departure slot. Pass `-Seed` to choose a different ordinary slot and `-SaveOutput` to choose a separate save destination. For a native input receipt add a new `-Sample` name and a mode. `entry` plays the east scroll and saves first control. `escape` plays to Bowie's EGRESS. `retry` starts from the saved Hassan return, raises the party and travels back. `win` plays from the supplied battle to its actual outcome. `continue` opens the title, selects the saved journey and moves. The private handoff identifies endpoint files and observed results for each language.

**Unknown** for this chapter includes human visual and audio acceptance, original hardware timing, promoted Janet, an alternate first active victory member in ordinary play, a nonempty depot on this branch, and completed higher-level rematches requiring a replacement enemy outside the mounted candidate set. Effective level 34 is the first upgrade threshold. The unmounted replacement path reports its existing unsupported result. Partial fights, refused saves and observer failures remain in their original receipts.
