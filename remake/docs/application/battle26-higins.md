The second starts the Chinese departure and plays to first battle control. The third checks a saved battle through the title. Use the package handoff for victory, retreat, ring and Relief replay.

```powershell
./Play.ps1 -Language en
./Play.ps1 -Language zh-CN -Mode entry -Sample entry
./Play.ps1 -Language en -Mode battle-continue -Sample battle -Seed ./slots/battle-en.json
```
