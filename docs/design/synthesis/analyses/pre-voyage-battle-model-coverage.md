# What the numbers already explain about the pre-voyage battles

For a first visit on Normal (difficulty 0), **all seven encounters have documented enemy placements, entry conditions and outcomes**. Only Battle01 has a dedicated [design analysis](battle-design-analysis-pilot.md), and that analysis is qualitative. **None of the seven has a complete encounter model** that connects an explicit allied loadout to legal first contact, damage in both directions, healing or action cost, and a tactical choice. The missing work is largely joining existing evidence. For example, Battle02 already gives the Huge Rat's *equipped, difficulty-adjusted* ATT, while another source gives a newly recruited Jaha's DEF. One comparison below shows what those numbers can tell a player.

The scope is the original USA release. **Confirmed** refers to facts or bounded observations at the linked owners, **Inferred** to a conditional design reading, and **Unknown** to inputs or consequences those owners leave open. The [remake campaign](../../../../remake/docs/application/pre-voyage-campaign.md) has useful win, loss, recovery and resource examples from its own runs. It verifies demo behavior. The original's encounter sheets and rules below supply the evidence for interpreting the source game.

## What the available evidence buys

- **Rules and terrain.** [Physical resolution](../../contracts/battle/combat-resolution.md) gives integer damage stages and bounded dodge, critical, repeat and counter cases. In its airborne-target example, a non-archer ground attacker rolls against a hovering target with range 8; a zero roll skips damage altogether. A hit estimate for a particular matchup must first select the applicable branch. [Spell resolution](../../contracts/battle/spell-resolution.md) covers separate spell/effect cases. [Battlefield navigation](../../contracts/battle/battlefield-navigation.md) distinguishes movement-type costs, occupied cells and attack range. Each proposed first contact needs the battle's terrain cell, mover and legal endpoint.
- **Allied strength.** [Progression and economy](../themes/progression-and-economy.md#party-development) connects joining, equipment, spell learning and growth. The [controlled opening party](../../../../remake/reference/inputs/map3-opening-party.json) and [Battle02 reference inputs](../../../../remake/reference/inputs/battle01-to-battle02-party.json) declare exact starting operands and their provenance. A template count is capacity. Which members actually fight, their level-ups, equipment, remaining items and statuses must come from a named history or declared scenario. **Unknown:** the distribution of ordinary players' levels, purchases and damage exposure at these battles.
- **Turn pressure and outcomes.** The encounter research identifies which AI commands, follow targets, territories and queue entries are possible. Outcome guides identify the goal, defeat, escape and special scripts. What remains to be connected is the number of **reachable hostile actions before the next recovery opportunity** under a chosen position, target and turn order. Turn slots, successful hits and full rounds measure different things.

## One paper calibration for Battle02

Use newly recruited, living Jaha as a starting point because his Level 1 reference row specifies 9 HP, ATT 14 and DEF 7 with his Short Axe already equipped. A fresh Huge Rat has 10 HP, DEF 8 and Normal ATT 12. For this **Inferred** example, assume a legal ordinary physical hit in each direction, full starting HP, a target land-effect setting of 0, no critical, extra strike, counter, healing or status change. No level is imputed to Bowie, Sarah or Chester. [Jaha reference row](../../../../remake/reference/inputs/battle01-to-battle02-party.json) · [Rat refresh](../../../research/chapters/grans-opening/battle02-initialization-and-first-control.md#enemy-refresh-entity-load-and-activation) · [Damage stages](../../contracts/battle/combat-resolution.md#2-calculate-base-physical-damage-with-integer-arithmetic) · [RNG range](../../../research/battle/runtime-rng-and-battle-math.md)

| Conditional exchange | Arithmetic | Tactical implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jaha hits the Rat | 14 − 8 = 6. The downward-spread range is 1, so both draws contribute zero. From 10 HP the Rat has 4 HP left. | Two such hits defeat the Rat. A reachable ally able to deal the remaining 4 could free Jaha's next action. |
| The Rat hits Jaha | 12 − 7 = 5. The same spread rule leaves 5 damage. From 9 HP Jaha has 4 HP left. | A second equivalent hit defeats him before another attacker has to join in. |
| The Rat hits Jaha with land-effect setting 1 on Jaha's tile | Floor(5 × 230 / 256) = 4 before spread. Two such hits leave 1 HP. | One defensive tile can buy a healing opportunity **if** Jaha can get there and the next ally can act in time. |

These are counts of clean landed hits at specified settings. The source-linked turn, dodge and terrain rules still apply. **Unknown:** whether this exposure exists on the first approach and whether Sarah gets an action in time. The next useful step is one Battle02 case: name a legal tile pair, Jaha's place in the actual roster, the Rat's active order and one reachable support action. Derive those two exchanges and compare focus fire with HEAL or withdrawal at that point. A documented exception or a second position that changes the answer is enough to judge whether to repeat the method for Battle03.
