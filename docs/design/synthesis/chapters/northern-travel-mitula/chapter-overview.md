# Northern Travel through First Pacalon — Chapter Overview

- **Story span (first half of Northern Travel only):** Picking up directly from the Dwarven Mine's
  **Cannon** gift ([Southern Travel, Caravan, and Creed — Chapter Overview](../southern-travel-creed/chapter-overview.md)),
  this overview covers returning to **New Granseal** (**Astral**, the **Inventor's Dynamite**, and
  **Janet**), the **North Parmecia** road (`Battle24`), blasting **North Cliff** and formally
  recruiting **Rohde**, crossing **North Cave** (`Battle25`), visiting **Ketto** (merchant
  relocation and Church), **Geshp's** ambush (`Battle26`) and rescuing **Higins**, and exploring
  **drought-state Pacalon Town and Castle** (including resident **Frayja**), ending at the
  admitted south departure onto the drought overworld before `Battle27`.
- **Chapter arc & world changes:** Reporting Elis's survival at New Granseal Castle brings
  **Astral** along as a persistent adviser, lets the town inventor pair your **Cannon** with
  **Dynamite**, and recruits **Janet** → clearing the northern road (`Battle24`) and blasting
  **North Cliff** consumes both explosives, formally recruits **Rohde**, and opens **North Cave**
  (`Battle25`) → reaching **Ketto** relocates its shopkeepers behind their counters and offers a
  northern Church return anchor → defeating Geshp's ambush (`Battle26`) rescues **Higins** and
  opens the road into **drought-state Pacalon Town and Castle**, leaving the force ready to head
  north toward Tristan and Mitula.
- **Outside this B3 overview (not covered here):** **`Battle27`**, **Pangoat Valley Bridge**
  (`Battle28`), **Tristan**, **Mitula's Shrine** (`Battle29`–`Battle30`), **restoring rain**
  (and rainy Pacalon), **`Battle31`**, and **later Frayja following or recruitment** lie **beyond
  this overview** (and remain **outside the playable remake frontier**). Follow the onward
  research sections in
  [Story Progression · Northern travel, Pacalon, Tristan and Mitula](../../themes/story-progression.md#northern-travel-pacalon-tristan-and-mitula)
  and the three post-Pacalon `Battle27` segment guides linked at the bottom.

## Dependency-ordered story events & branches (player view)

*Three non-obvious rules govern this route:*
- **Three independent New Granseal triggers:** The castle audience with **Astral** does **not**
  require holding the Cannon; the **Inventor's Dynamite** requires both the completed audience and
  a carried Cannon; **Janet's** recruitment requires only the completed audience (not Dynamite,
  Elric, or active-party room).
- **Carried packs vs. Caravan Depot:** Both the Inventor and North Cliff scan **joined packs**
  (reserves and fallen allies count), **never** the Caravan Depot.
- **No fixed retreat town:** Defeat or `EGRESS` in `Battle24`, `Battle25`, and `Battle26` always
  uses your **currently saved Church return town** (for example, Hassan until you confirm Save at
  Ketto or Pacalon)—none of these encounters hard-code North Cliff or Pacalon.

## Key items, pickups & the Cannon / Dynamite chain

*Detailed custody, drop, and equipment rules live in
[campaign-items.md](../../../../../remake/docs/content/campaign-items.md).*

### B3 story-item chain (each check has its own custody scope)

1. **Cannon (carried from B2 → `N2` → `N5`):** Received strictly in Bowie's 4-slot pack at the
   Dwarven Mine (Phase B2). For **Dynamite (`N2`)** and **North Cliff (`N5`)**, **any joined
   member's pack** counts (including reserves and fallen members), while **Caravan Depot storage
   does NOT count**. The Inventor leaves the Cannon untouched; North Cliff removes one Cannon
   together with Dynamite.
2. **Dynamite (`N2` → `N5`):** Granted by the New Granseal Inventor after the castle audience
   (`N1`) while Cannon is carried in joined packs; placed into the **first joined member with a
   free pack slot** (full party refuses cleanly for retry). Consumed alongside Cannon at North
   Cliff (`N5`).
3. **Missing-item refusal & retry trap (`N5`):** Missing **either** Cannon or Dynamite at North
   Cliff plays dialogue naming **Dynamite**, consumes neither item, and sets a **same-visit
   latch**. Because an overworld border scroll retains that latch, retrieving a stored Cannon from
   the Caravan Depot requires **entering a town (e.g., New Granseal) and walking back out** before
   North Cliff will test your packs again.

## Evidence & boundary rules

- **Original:** All Northern Travel through first Pacalon segments (`N1`–`N9`) are **Confirmed
  static** in pinned `SF2DISASM` (`c834c652b6862bc5679fd7f69a38a7093206efc6`) and the USA retail
  ROM ([Cannon & North Cliff coverage](../../../../research/story-content-coverage.md#returning-for-the-cannon-and-opening-north-cliff),

  Original runtime observations stop earlier at `Map3`–`Battle01`; natural original traversal,
  hardware timing, rendered presentation, and original SRAM persistence remain **Unknown**.
- **Remake:** Ordinary continuous bilingual play and cold Save/Continue cover New Granseal return
  (Astral, Dynamite, Janet), `Battle24`, North Cliff blast & Rohde (including Depot refusal and
  fresh-entry retry), `Battle25` & North Cave (`Fairy Tear` field Search), Ketto (merchant
  relocation, Church Save/Cancel, shops, pickups), `Battle26` & Higins, and drought-state Pacalon
  Town & Castle (centaur/stairs, resident Frayja, independent Town & Castle Churches, pickups,
  promotions, and controlled `Map71` departure)
  ([capability status](../../../../../remake/docs/capability-status.md#campaign-feature-trace)).
  **`Battle27`, Tristan, Mitula, restored-rain Pacalon (`F530`), `Battle31`, and later Frayja
  recruitment are NOT yet playable in the remake** (see
  [remaining campaign baseline](../../../../../remake/docs/application/remaining-campaign-risk-baseline.md)).
- **Original Unknown ≠ remake missing:** An **Unknown** in an original segment guide marks the
  limit of original-game runtime evidence, not an unimplemented remake feature, and a passing remake
  observation does not prove original-console chronology or timing.
