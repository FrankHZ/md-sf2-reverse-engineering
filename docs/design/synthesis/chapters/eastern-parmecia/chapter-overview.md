# New Granseal and Eastern Parmecia — Chapter Overview

- **Story span:** Picking up directly from Grans departure ([Grans Chapter Overview](../grans-opening/chapter-overview.md)),
  this chapter runs from the sea voyage and rough landing on Parmecia through the damaged-ship
  rescue (`Battle08`), founding **New Granseal**, meeting **Peter**, traveling east through
  **Ribble**, the **Dark Caves**, **Polca**, **Mount Volcano**, and **Bedoe / Volcanon**
  (`Battle09`–`Battle13`), and ends once Bowie boards Polca's **raft** and takes the **first
  controlled move across water**.
- **Chapter arc & world changes:** Refugees cross the sea, fight to rescue a trapped worker
  (`Battle08`), and dismantle their damaged ship to build New Granseal → one year later, the
  talking phoenix **Peter** sparks an eastward expedition through Ribble (`Battle09`–`10`) and the
  Dark Caves (`Battle11`) → in Polca and Bedoe (`Battle12`), **Volcanon** refuses direct aid and
  Peter commits to fighting under player control → returning to defend Polca (`Battle13`) recruits
  **Gerhalt**, secures the mayor's **raft**, takes **Oddler** along, recruits **Luke**, and opens
  water travel toward southern Parmecia.
- **What continues later (outside this chapter):** River navigation, disembarking, the desert
  battle (`Battle14`), the Kraken (`Battle16`), **Hassan**, the **Wooden Panel / Achilles Sword**
  chain, and the **Caravan** belong to the next chapter (`#2366` Phase B2; see
  [Story Progression](../../themes/story-progression.md#southern-travel-caravan-and-creed)).

## Chronological story events & branches (player view)

*Events below are grouped by **story dependency**, not forced into one rigid checklist. Optional
branches stay beside their real place and timing limits:*

- **No blanket "revisitable until chapter end" rule:** each optional event or pickup depends on its
  own town state or later milestone—for example, helping Polca's turtle closes once you visit
  **Volcanon** (`E14`), and rescuing Bedoe's hatchling closes once you recruit **Elric** later in
  Southern Travel (`Battle20`).
- **East Shrine is not a Dark Caves gate:** walking straight from the `Battle10` victory bank to
  East Shrine is not established on foot; skipping it never blocks the Dark Caves (`E11`).

## Key items, pickups & the raft chain

*Eastern Parmecia has **no mandatory inventory key-item gate**. The **raft** is a multi-step
transport state—not a pack item—and later Parmecia story items (**Wooden Panel**, **Achilles
Sword**, **Caravan**) belong to `#2366` Phase B2 rather than this chapter's main path
([campaign-items.md](../../../../../remake/docs/content/campaign-items.md)).*

### Raft progression chain (five distinct steps)

1. **Mayor's bargain (`E17`):** Four talks in one post-`Battle13` Polca visit grant **raft
   ownership** (still on foot inside town).
2. **Stored shore berth (`E17`):** The same completion writes the raft's **parked overworld coast
   location** south of Polca (a separate piece of state from ownership; old battle saves without a
   berth can hold ownership while leaving the parked spot unknown).
3. **Physical raft on the overworld (`E19`):** Entering the coast outside Polca spawns the
   **parked raft actor** at that stored berth (separate from Luke's join scene).
4. **Aligned boarding (`E20`):** Walking onto the exact water tile holding the parked raft enters
   **raft riding mode** (approaching any other water tile leaves you on foot).
5. **First controlled water move (`E20`):** Moving across open water completes Chapter B1;
   disembarking, river warps, and `Battle16` (Kraken) begin in Phase B2.

## Evidence & boundary rules

- **Original:** Every Eastern Parmecia segment (`E1`–`E20`) is **Confirmed static** in pinned
  `SF2DISASM` (`c834c652b6862bc5679fd7f69a38a7093206efc6`) and the USA retail ROM
  ([story coverage](../../../../research/story-content-coverage.md#eastern-parmecia-ribble-polca-and-bedoe)).
  Original runtime observations stop earlier at `Map3`–`Battle01`; natural original walking,
  earliest original pre-raft East Shrine access, hardware timing, and original SRAM persistence
  remain **Unknown**.
- **Remake:** Ordinary continuous play and field/battle saves cover the main route from the
  damaged ship (`Battle08`) through New Granseal, Ribble (with/without May), `Battle09`–`13`,
  Bedoe/Volcanon, hatchling flight/rescue, Oddler's branches, Luke's join, boarding the parked raft,
  and taking the first water move ([capability status](../../../../../remake/docs/capability-status.md#campaign-feature-trace),
  [`polca-first-raft.md`](../../../../../remake/docs/application/polca-first-raft.md)). Separate
  optional or controlled checks (such as the pre-raft East Shrine bank-side probe, Polca fairy
  rescue run, and `F13`-supplied hatchling check) do not upgrade those sub-routes into one proved
  natural path.
- **Original Unknown ≠ remake missing:** An **Unknown** in an original segment guide marks the
  limit of original-game runtime evidence, not an unimplemented remake feature.
- **Publication note:** Maintained in the canonical repository first; live Wiki synchronization
  happens separately after acceptance.
