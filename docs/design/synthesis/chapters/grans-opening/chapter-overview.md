# Grans Opening and Departure — Chapter Overview

- **Story span:** Ordinary New Game (waking up at Bowie's home in Granseal) through confirming
  departure with the Captain at Granseal Docks to sail for Parmecia. The opening storm and temple
  prologue provides background only; the sea voyage, landing, and `Battle08` continue in
  [Voyage and landing](voyage-through-damaged-ship-control.md) and
  [Damaged-ship plank through Battle08](../eastern-parmecia/damaged-ship-plank-through-battle08.md).
- **Chapter arc & world changes:** A peaceful town morning and first Ancient Tower battle
  (`Battle01`) → King Granseal's commission and western search through Yeel and Hawel's house
  (`Battle02`–`03`) → Galam ambush, captivity, prison escape, and recovering the **Jewel of Light**
  (`Battle04`–`06`) → returning to invaded Granseal, descending the Ancient Tower to seize the
  **Jewel of Evil** (`Battle07`), escaping the town-shattering earthquake, and evacuating by ship.

## Chronological story events (player view)

*Optional stops and pickups stay available while their owning town or field area remains reachable
in that world state. Two major turning points close earlier areas or casts: **winning `Battle07`**
triggers the great earthquake that replaces Granseal with the ruined evacuation town (closing
invaded Granseal's town cast—including Kiwi—and the west road back to Yeel, Hawel, Galam, and the
southern shrine), and **confirming departure at the docks (`G20`)** closes Grans exploration until
late in the campaign.*

## Evidence & boundary rules

- **Original:** Only the `Map3`–`Battle01` opening has bounded original runtime observations
  ([original frontier](../../milestones/map3-battle01-readiness.md#accepted-original-frontier)).
  Later Grans events (`Battle02`–`07`, earthquake, docks) are **Confirmed static** in pinned
  `SF2DISASM` (`c834c652b6862bc5679fd7f69a38a7093206efc6`) and the USA retail ROM; natural original
  traversal, hardware timing, and original SRAM persistence remain **Unknown**.
- **Remake:** Ordinary continuous play and v6 settled saves cover New Game through `Map14` first
  control ([pre-voyage campaign](../../../../../remake/docs/application/pre-voyage-campaign.md)).
  The opening storm/temple acts (`--opening-act 1–4`) are background scenes outside `G1`–`G20`.
  Named controlled Debug starts (such as `slade-join` and `castle-quake`) verify specific scene
  transitions and **do not** prove a continuous natural playthrough or carry earned gold/EXP.
- **Flags are not the whole session:** Story flags (`Fnnn`) select setups and gates, but live
  actors, follower links, working layout copies, and party resources also depend on how the map was
  entered or restored.
- **Publication note:** Maintained in the canonical repository first; live Wiki synchronization
  happens separately after acceptance.
